the third voice

Sound two tones together and a third appears — a real frequency, present in neither. Giuseppe Tartini heard it in 1714. Your ear makes it from the gap.

Drag the amber light and the blue light to change their pitch. Find the space between sameness (a drone) and too-far-apart (noise) — the band where the third voice rings.
